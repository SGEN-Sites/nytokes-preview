Dispensary Guide for Rockland County

Rockland County has far fewer licensed dispensaries than its population would suggest, and the reason is that New York let every city, town, and village decide for itself whether to allow retail cannabis. Plenty of Rockland municipalities said no. That is why a Nanuet or New City shopper usually ends up driving to a neighboring village rather than buying at home.

Chapter 02

Why are there so few dispensaries in Rockland County?

Under New York’s Marijuana Regulation and Taxation Act, cities, towns, and villages had until the end of 2021 to opt out of allowing adult-use retail dispensaries inside their borders. A municipality that opted out cannot host a retail storefront. One that did not opt out can, subject to state licensing and local zoning.

A number of Rockland municipalities opted out, which is why the licensed storefronts cluster in the places that did not. The state keeps the current list, and the Office of Cannabis Management publishes it along with the licensing rules. If someone tells you a shop in an opted-out town is licensed, check it yourself in the state’s dispensary verification tool before you spend money there.

Unlicensed shops are still around in Rockland, and they look convincing. Dispensary versus smoke shop covers how to tell them apart in about thirty seconds, and the short version is that a licensed store posts its OCM license number and tests everything it sells. Toke’s are OCM-RETL-25-000418 and OCM-RETL-25-000344.

Chapter 03

Where do Nanuet shoppers buy legal weed?

Nanuet sits roughly four miles east of Spring Valley, and the drive is a straight run on Route 59 past the Nanuet Mall area. Call it ten minutes in normal traffic and closer to twenty on a Saturday afternoon when Route 59 backs up near the shopping corridor.

Nanuet also has a Pascack Valley Line station two stops from Spring Valley, which matters if you commute. Most people drive, and parking at the S Central Ave store is off-street. If you would rather skip the trip, the Nanuet delivery page has the coverage details.

Chapter 04

Getting to a dispensary from Nyack

Nyack is about eight miles from Spring Valley, east on Route 59 through West Nyack and then down toward the river. Fifteen to twenty minutes, traffic depending, and the Palisades Center in West Nyack is roughly the halfway point if you are combining errands.

The river villages run together here. Upper Nyack and South Nyack pull from the same handful of roads, and neither has a licensed dispensary of its own at the time of writing, which leaves Spring Valley or delivery. The Nyack delivery page covers the village and the area right around it.

Chapter 05

Monsey is about two miles from the Spring Valley store

Monsey is the closest of the four to S Central Ave. Take Route 59 west or Route 306 south and you are there in five or six minutes. For a lot of Monsey shoppers the store is closer than their supermarket.

Proximity is also why the kosher-friendly page exists rather than being a marketing gesture. Certification questions come up constantly at this counter, and the staff can tell you which products on the menu carry which certification instead of guessing. The Monsey delivery page handles the rest.

Chapter 06

New City shoppers: the drive down Route 304

New City is about seven miles north of Spring Valley, fifteen minutes or so on Route 45 or Route 304 depending on where you start. Coming from the Congers or Valley Cottage side, cutting over to Route 304 beats backtracking through New City center.

It is the county seat, so a lot of people are already driving past on a weekday for something else. If you are not, the New City delivery page has the coverage specifics.

Chapter 07

Does Toke deliver in Rockland County?

Yes, out of the Spring Valley store. The minimum is $75. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. You have to be the person who placed the order, you have to be there at the door, and you need a government photo ID. Drivers take cash or debit, never a credit card, because federal law keeps card networks out of cannabis entirely.

Town pages with the coverage specifics: Spring Valley, Suffern, Pearl River, West Nyack, Pomona, Congers, Valley Cottage, Chestnut Ridge, Airmont, Hillcrest, Orangeburg, Tappan, Blauvelt, Haverstraw, and Stony Point.

Chapter 08

What the Spring Valley store is like to walk into

It is a counter store, not a warehouse. You come in, you show ID at the door, and a budtender walks the menu with you. Nobody is working a commission, so if you ask what is good this week you will get an actual opinion, including the ones that start with “not that, it has been sitting.”

Two things about this store specifically. Spanish is spoken at the counter, which is what the Se Habla Español page is about, and the hours run to 11:00 PM, half an hour later than the Yonkers store. If you have never been in a licensed dispensary before, what to expect on your first visit covers the ID check, the limits, and what the counter conversation is actually like.

Chapter 09

What can you legally buy in Rockland County?

The same thing you can buy anywhere in New York: up to 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate per day, if you are 21 or older with a valid government photo ID. Flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, drinks, and concentrates are all legal here, and everything on a licensed shelf has been lab tested and tracked from the farm. New York cannabis laws explained walks through the limits, the taxes that get added at the register, and where you can legally consume.

Chapter 10

Frequently asked questions

Is there a dispensary in Rockland County, NY? Yes. Toke is at 2 S Central Ave in Spring Valley, licensed by the New York Office of Cannabis Management and open daily from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Several Rockland municipalities opted out of retail cannabis, so licensed storefronts are concentrated in the places that did not.

Is there a dispensary in Nanuet? Nanuet shoppers generally drive to Spring Valley, about four miles west on Route 59, or order delivery. Check the state’s dispensary verification tool before buying from any shop advertising itself as a Nanuet dispensary.

How far is Spring Valley from Nyack? About eight miles, or fifteen to twenty minutes east on Route 59 through West Nyack. Delivery to Nyack is the alternative if you would rather not drive.

Does Toke deliver to Monsey and New City? Yes. Delivery runs out of the Spring Valley store across Rockland, with a $75 minimum and the fee confirmed at checkout by zip. Monsey is roughly two miles away and New City about seven.

Do I need a medical card to buy cannabis in Rockland County? No. Adult-use cannabis is legal for anyone 21 or older with a valid government photo ID. New York medical cardholders get 10% off at Toke, but a card is not required to shop.

How do I know a Rockland dispensary is licensed? Look it up in the state’s dispensary verification tool, and look for a posted OCM license number in the store. Toke holds OCM-RETL-25-000418 and OCM-RETL-25-000344.
