Do you deliver cannabis to Tuckahoe?

Yes. NY Tokes delivers cannabis to Tuckahoe, Westchester County from our licensed Yonkers dispensary. Add to cart at the online menu and your Tuckahoe zip will confirm coverage and delivery window at checkout.

Is NY Tokes a licensed dispensary serving Tuckahoe?

Yes. NY Tokes holds a New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license and is fully licensed to deliver to Tuckahoe and the rest of Westchester County. Every product is lab-tested, METRC-tracked from seed to sale, and 100% NYS-compliant. There are still unlicensed delivery services floating around Westchester, we're not one of them.

How fast is Tuckahoe cannabis delivery?

Pick ASAP or a scheduled window at checkout and you'll see the ETA before you pay. Order earlier in the day for the widest choice of windows. You get a real ETA and a driver text, no all-day "out for delivery" guesses.

Is there a minimum order for Tuckahoe delivery?

There's a $75 delivery minimum. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. Add a few items to cart at our online menu and your zip confirms coverage at checkout.

Do I need a medical card to order delivery in Tuckahoe?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21+ in Tuckahoe with a valid government-issued photo ID at the door. NYS medical cardholders get 10% off every Tuckahoe delivery on top of any current promotion.

How do I pay for Tuckahoe delivery?

Cash, debit, Dutchie Pay, AeroPay or Alleaves Pay. No credit cards. Federal banking rules prevent credit-card transactions at any New York dispensary or delivery service. Some Tuckahoe drivers carry portable PIN-debit terminals, confirm at checkout.

How much cannabis can I buy in one Tuckahoe delivery order?

Adult-use limits in New York are 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate per day. Our menu and checkout will keep you within the legal limit automatically.

What are your Tuckahoe delivery hours?

Tuckahoe delivery runs every day, generally 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, including weekends and most holidays. Order cutoffs and last-call windows can shift around 4/20, Christmas, and Thanksgiving, check Instagram @nytokes for live updates.
