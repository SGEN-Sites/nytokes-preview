What Counts as Kosher in the Cannabis Aisle

Cannabis is a plant, and plants are naturally kosher. Where it actually matters is in the ingredients added to edibles, gummies, drinks, and infused products. Here's the row-by-row cheat sheet, plus what to ask for at the counter.

  • Flower & Pre-Rolls Naturally kosher Whole flower is just the plant. No added ingredients, no animal derivatives. Pre-rolls with paper wraps are fine. Skip the infused wraps if you want to keep it strict.
  • Edibles & Chocolates Check the symbol The category that matters most. Look for OU, OK, Star-K, Kof-K, CRC, or another recognized hechsher right on the package. Several NY-licensed edible brands carry certification. We'll point you to them.
  • Gummies & Soft Chews Check the symbol Gelatin is usually the gotcha. Pectin-based gummies are the better bet. Certified brands print the hechsher on the package, no guessing.
  • THC Beverages Check the symbol Seltzers and tonics are simple. Water, THC emulsion, flavors. Several beverages we stock carry kosher certification right on the can.
  • Vapes & Concentrates Naturally kosher Distillate, live resin, rosin. All plant-derived, no animal product involved. Considered acceptable under most kosher standards. Check with your rabbi for the strictest tier.
  • Topicals & Tinctures Ask first Glycerin and carrier oils can be plant or animal. Look for the cert mark, or ask us. Certified brands tell you exactly what's in the carrier.

Kosher Cannabis FAQ

Quick answers to what we hear most often from Spring Valley customers. Don't see yours? Call (845) 406-3004.

Is cannabis kosher?

Cannabis flower itself is a plant, and plants are naturally kosher. Where it gets nuanced is with edibles, gummies, chocolates, drinks, and anything infused, because that's where added ingredients (gelatin, flavorings, glycerin) can change the answer. Look for a kosher symbol on the package, or just ask us.

Do you stock OU, OK, Star-K, or Kof-K certified products?

Yes. Several brands on our Spring Valley shelf carry one of the recognized hechshers. Inventory rotates, so the move is to ask any budtender what's currently certified. We'll show you the symbol on the packaging.

Are your gummies kosher?

Some are, some aren't. It depends on the brand and whether they use gelatin (animal-derived) or pectin (plant-based). Pectin-based gummies from certified brands are usually the simplest path. Tell us at the counter and we'll point you there.

Is smoking flower considered kosher?

Cannabis flower is plant matter with no animal derivatives, so it's broadly considered acceptable. Different rabbinical authorities take different views on cannabis use itself. That's a conversation for your rabbi, not your budtender. What we can tell you is what's on the shelf.

Do you carry anything Passover-friendly (kosher for Pesach)?

Some certified edible brands publish a Passover-acceptable list each year. Selection changes seasonally. Call ahead or swing by closer to the holiday and we'll tell you exactly what's available that week.
