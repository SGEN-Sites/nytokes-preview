Chapter 01

First Things First: Is Cannabis Legal in New York?

Yes. Adult-use cannabis is legal in New York for anyone 21 and over.

That means if you’re 21+ and shopping at a licensed dispensary like Toke Cannabis, you’re good to go.

What’s not legal is buying from:

  • Unlicensed smoke shops
  • Random bodegas
  • “Pop-up” sellers with no state approval

That’s one of the biggest reasons people in Yonkers and Spring Valley choose Toke New York, you know exactly what you’re buying and where it came from.

Chapter 03

What You Can’t Buy (Even Though People Ask Us Constantly)

Let’s clear up the most common misconceptions we hear in Yonkers and Spring Valley:

Cannabis if You’re Under 21

Doesn’t matter if you “left your ID in the car”: New York law is strict here.

Super-High-Dose Edibles

Those 500mg gummies you saw online? Not legal in NY dispensaries.

Anything Unlabeled or Untested

If there’s no lab testing, batch number, and THC breakdown, it’s not compliant.

Buying More Than the Legal Limit

New York sets possession limits, and licensed dispensaries follow them closely.

At Toke Cannabis, we’d rather explain the rules clearly than sell you something that puts you at risk.

Chapter 05

Where Can You Consume Cannabis in New York?

Another daily question.

Generally speaking:

  • If smoking tobacco is allowed, cannabis usually is too
  • Private residences are fine (unless the landlord says otherwise)
  • Cars = nope (even parked)

Public consumption laws can still feel confusing, so if you’re unsure, just ask us in-store. That’s literally what we’re here for.
