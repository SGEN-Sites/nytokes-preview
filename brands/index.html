How we pick

We hand-pick our menu to offer high-quality options at prices that make sense, because great cannabis should be accessible to everyone. More options doesn't mean better options, so we only bring in products we would:

  • Recommend to our friends
  • Smoke ourselves
  • Stand behind when you come back next week

Brands FAQ

Do both stores carry the same brands?

Not always. Availability changes by store and by week, so check the Yonkers or Spring Valley menu to see what's in stock right now.

Why don't you carry every brand I've seen online?

Two reasons. A lot of out-of-state brands simply aren't allowed in New York, which we explain in why New York cannabis is different. And we curate on purpose: if it doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't make the shelf.

Is everything on the menu lab-tested?

Yes. Every product is lab-tested, METRC-tracked, and 100% NYS-compliant. Learn what lab-tested actually means.
