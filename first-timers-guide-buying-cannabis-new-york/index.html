Chapter 01

Start here

If you’ve never walked into a licensed dispensary, it’s a lot less complicated than people expect. You show your ID, you talk to a budtender, you pick something, you pay. That’s it.

This guide walks through what to bring, what to ask, and how ordering and delivery work at our two stores, so the first visit feels like the tenth.

Chapter 02

What to bring

  • A valid government photo ID. You have to be 21 or older, and we check every time.
  • A way to pay. No dispensary in New York can take credit cards. At Yonkers we take cash, debit, Dutchie Pay, AeroPay and Alleaves Pay. At Spring Valley it’s cash or debit.
  • A rough idea of what you want the night to look like. You don’t need to know strain names. “Something mellow for a movie” is a better starting point than a THC number.

Chapter 03

What happens when you walk in

We check your ID before you shop. After that you’re on the floor with the menu and a budtender. Tell them it’s your first time. That’s the most useful thing you can say, and nobody is going to rush you.

A good budtender will ask how you plan to use it (smoke, vape, eat or drink), how much experience you have, and what kind of experience you’re after. Then they’ll show you two or three options instead of the whole wall. If you want to know more about how that conversation goes, we wrote about it in how our budtenders help you find the right product.

Chapter 04

Picking your first product

The main formats are flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles and drinks. Each one works differently, and the label tells you what’s in it. A few things first-timers tend to ask about:

  • Pre-rolls are ready to go. No grinding or rolling.
  • Edibles and drinks take longer to kick in than smoking, and effects vary a lot from person to person. Start low, wait, and don’t stack a second serving because you “don’t feel anything yet.”
  • THC percentage isn’t the whole story. A higher number doesn’t mean a better experience. Our post on what THC percentage actually means breaks it down.

Not sure how to read the package? Our guide to reading a New York cannabis label walks through every part of it.

Chapter 05

Pickup and delivery

You can order ahead on the Yonkers menu or the Spring Valley menu and pick up at the counter, or have it delivered.

  • Delivery has a $75 minimum. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more.
  • Yonkers delivers to lower Westchester County. Spring Valley delivers to all of Rockland County. We don’t deliver to the Bronx or New York City.
  • The person who placed the order has to be there to accept it, 21+ with a valid photo ID.
Chapter 06

A few ground rules

  • Buy from licensed stores only. You can check any shop in the state’s licensed dispensary verification tool.
  • Don’t drive after using cannabis.
  • Store it out of reach of kids and pets.
  • Don’t use it in public places where smoking isn’t allowed.
