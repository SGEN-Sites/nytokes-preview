FAQ · straight answers

Questions we hear behind the counter

Every answer below comes from Toke’s own store, rewards and delivery pages. Don’t see yours? Call Yonkers at 914-207-1545 or Spring Valley at 845-406-3004.

From the Yonkers store page

Yonkers store

Open the full page

Is NY Tokes a licensed Yonkers dispensary?

Yes. NY Tokes holds New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000418. Every product on our shelves is lab-tested, METRC-tracked from seed to sale, and 100% compliant with NYS law. If you've seen unlicensed shops around Yonkers, this isn't one of them, we're the real deal.

Where is the Yonkers dispensary located?

We're at 471 McLean Ave, Yonkers, NY 10705, on the south side of Yonkers right near the Bronx border. We're easy to reach from Bronxville, Mount Vernon and most of southern Westchester.

Do I need a medical card to shop?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21+ with a valid government-issued ID. We also offer a 10% discount for registered NYS medical cannabis patients with a valid card.

What time does the Yonkers location open and close?

We're open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, including weekends and most holidays. Holiday hours can shift; check our Instagram @nytokes for live updates.

Do you deliver to the Bronx or NYC?

No. We deliver to Yonkers and lower Westchester from the Yonkers shop, including Yonkers ZIPs 10701, 10703, 10704, 10705 and 10710 and White Plains. Add to cart online and your zip will tell you if you're in coverage.

How much cannabis can I buy in one visit?

Adult-use customers can purchase up to 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate per day under New York State law. Our staff will help you stay within your limit and explain anything you're not sure about.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Credit cards aren't accepted at NY dispensaries due to federal banking regulations. We take cash, debit, Dutchie Pay, AeroPay and Alleaves Pay, and there's an ATM in the shop with low fees. Most regulars bring cash to keep it simple.

What's the best time to come in?

Mornings (10am-noon) and late evenings (8pm to close) are usually the chillest. Weekends get busy after 4pm, order ahead online and skip the line.

Is there parking near 471 McLean Ave?

Yes, there's street parking on McLean Avenue and the side streets. Most customers find a spot within a block. We don't have a dedicated lot, but turnover is steady.

Is NY Tokes a licensed dispensary in Spring Valley?

Yes. NY Tokes holds New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000344. Every product on our shelves is lab-tested, METRC-tracked from seed to sale, and fully compliant with NYS cannabis law. There are still unlicensed shops floating around Rockland, we're not one of them.

Where is the Spring Valley dispensary located?

We're at 2 S Central Ave, Spring Valley, NY 10977. Easy to reach from Route 59 and the Palisades Parkway, and central to Monsey, Nanuet, New City, Suffern, Pearl River and Nyack.

Do you deliver across Rockland County?

Yes. We deliver to most Rockland zip codes, including Monsey, Nanuet, New City, Suffern, Pearl River, West Nyack, Nyack, Pomona, Airmont, Chestnut Ridge and surrounding towns. Add to cart online and your zip will confirm coverage and the delivery window.

Do I need a medical marijuana card to shop?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID. NYS medical cardholders get 10% off every order on top of any current promotion.

What are your hours?

Open every day, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends and most holidays. Hours can shift on Christmas or Thanksgiving; check our Instagram @nytokes for the live update.

Can I order ahead for pickup or curbside?

Absolutely. Order online, choose pickup or curbside, and we'll have it ready when you arrive. Saves the line, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings.

How do I pay?

Cash, PIN-based debit, or our in-store ATM. Federal banking rules prevent credit-card transactions at any New York dispensary, our ATM has low fees.

How much cannabis can I buy in one trip?

Adult-use limits in New York are 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate per day. Our budtenders will help you stay within the rules and explain anything you're unsure about.

Is there parking at the Spring Valley shop?

Yes, there's plenty of parking right along Central Avenue. No meters, no garage, no stress.

What's nearby if I want to make an afternoon of it?

We're a short drive from the Palisades Center for shopping, Rockland Lake State Park for a hike, and dozens of restaurants along Route 59. Easy to combine the visit with the rest of your day.

How do I join Toke Rewards?

Create an account on our website. You're signed up automatically. On either shop page, tap Sign in, then Create account.

What do I get for signing up?

250 points, which is $5 off your next order.

How many points do I earn?

1 point for every $1 you spend before tax.

Are there other ways to earn points?

Yes. Download the Toke app and we'll add 250 bonus points to your account.

What are my points worth?

250 points is $5 off, and it goes up from there, all the way to 5,000 points for $185 off. Bigger redemptions are worth more per point.

Do my points expire?

No. Your points never expire.

Can I use my points at both stores?

Yes. It's one program across Toke Yonkers and Toke Spring Valley.

Can I combine points with other discounts?

Yes, with select discounts. Ask your budtender which ones apply.

How does the referral program work?

Share your referral link from your account. Your friend gets 100 points when they sign up, and you get 250 points when they place their first order over $10.

Is there a birthday reward?

You get 15% off on your birthday.

Who can join?

Anyone 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID.

Westchester delivery

Does NY Tokes deliver cannabis in Westchester County?

Yes. We run cannabis delivery across lower Westchester from the Yonkers shop, including places like Mount Vernon and Bronxville in the south up through the Rivertowns, White Plains and Chappaqua. Westchester delivery runs 10am to 10:30pm Sun: Thu, 10am to 11pm Fri: Sat. You can take ASAP delivery or book a scheduled hourly window between 10 AM and 10:30 PM (11 PM Fridays and Saturdays).

Which Westchester towns and zip codes are in the delivery zone?

The current Westchester zone covers Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, White Plains, Yonkers, Bronxville, Eastchester, Tuckahoe, Pelham, Pelham Manor, Scarsdale, Hartsdale, Hastings-on-Hudson, Dobbs Ferry, Ardsley, Irvington, Tarrytown, Sleepy Hollow, Ossining, Croton-on-Hudson, Larchmont, Mamaroneck, Harrison, Rye, Rye Brook, Port Chester, Purchase, Elmsford, Valhalla, Hawthorne, Thornwood, Pleasantville, Armonk, Chappaqua and more. Every town has its own page in the grid above. Add to cart online and your zip confirms coverage and the delivery window.

Is there a delivery minimum or a delivery fee in Westchester?

There's a $75 minimum on delivery orders. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. For help with your specific Westchester zone, call the Yonkers shop at (914) 207-1545 and a team member will walk you through it.

Where is the NY Tokes dispensary in Westchester?

Our Westchester store is at 471 McLean Ave, Yonkers, NY 10705, in southwest Yonkers a few blocks from the Bronx line. It's open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, and the phone number is (914) 207-1545. You can shop in store, order online for in-store pickup, pull up for curbside, or have it delivered.

What do I need to place a Westchester delivery order?

You need to be 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID, and the purchaser has to be there to accept the delivery. No medical card is required. Veterans get 15% off with a DD-214 or VA ID, and registered New York State medical patients get 10% off with a valid card.

Is NY Tokes a licensed New York dispensary?

Yes. The Yonkers store operates under New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000418, and our Spring Valley store operates under OCM-RETL-25-000344. Everything on the shelf and on the delivery menu is lab-tested and METRC-tracked from seed to sale.
