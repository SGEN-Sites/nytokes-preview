Where we actually serve

Two Stores, Two Counties, 80 Delivery Areas

NY Tokes is a New York licensed cannabis retailer with two stores in the lower Hudson Valley. 471 McLean Ave in Yonkers covers Westchester County. 2 S Central Ave in Spring Valley covers Rockland County. Between them, delivery reaches 80 towns, villages and hamlets, and every one of those areas has its own page with local details.

Both stores sell the same way: shop in person, order online for in-store pickup, pull up for curbside, or have it delivered. Delivery runs ASAP or on scheduled hourly windows, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM in Rockland and 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM (11:00 PM Fri: Sat) in Westchester, tracked in real time, in unmarked vehicles. There is a $75 delivery minimum. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more.

Veterans get 15% off every order. New York State registered medical patients get 10% off. Show proof once at either store and the team keeps it on file.

How far the footprint goes

The Hudson Valley is a big region and NY Tokes covers the southern end of it. Here is exactly where delivery runs today.

  • Rockland County: 40 delivery areas, served from the Spring Valley store.
  • Westchester County: 40 delivery areas, served from the Yonkers store.
  • Further north: Orange, Putnam, Dutchess, Ulster and the mid Hudson Valley are not in the current delivery footprint.

Enter your ZIP at checkout and the menu confirms your zone and delivery window before you pay.

Questions

Hudson Valley Cannabis FAQs

Does NY Tokes deliver cannabis in the Hudson Valley?

Yes, within two Hudson Valley counties. NY Tokes delivers across Rockland County and Westchester County from its two licensed stores in Spring Valley and Yonkers. Delivery does not currently run into Orange, Putnam, Dutchess or the counties further north. Enter your ZIP code at checkout and the menu confirms whether you sit inside a live delivery zone.

Which Hudson Valley counties does NY Tokes actually cover?

Rockland County and Westchester County, the two counties at the southern end of the Hudson Valley. There are 40 Rockland delivery areas and 40 Westchester delivery areas listed on this page, each with its own local page.

What is the delivery minimum and is there a delivery fee?

There is a $75 delivery minimum. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. Each delivery zone has its own required minimum, and you have to hit it before the order will go out.

How late can I order delivery?

In Rockland County, delivery runs 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM every day, ASAP or in scheduled hourly windows. In Westchester County, delivery runs 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, ASAP or in scheduled hourly windows.

Where are the NY Tokes stores in the Hudson Valley?

There are two. The Yonkers store is at 471 McLean Ave in Westchester County, open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday (license OCM-RETL-25-000418). The Spring Valley store is at 2 S Central Ave, Spring Valley NY 10977 in Rockland County, open Monday through Sunday, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM (license OCM-RETL-25-000344).

Can I pick up instead of getting delivery?

Yes. Both stores offer in-store pickup and curbside pickup. Order from the online menu, then either walk in and collect it or pull up outside and the team brings it out to your car.

Do veterans and medical patients get a discount?

Yes. Veterans get 15% off every order, and New York State registered medical patients get 10% off. Show proof once at either store and the team keeps it on file.
