Late Shift Snack Board

Crackers, cheese, fruit, something salty, something sweet. No stove, nothing infused. Built for the night you finally get home and plan to stay there.

Shop YonkersShop Spring Valley

The recipe

Makes
1 board, serves 2
Time
10 minutes
THC
None

Ingredients

  • Crackers, two kinds if you have them
  • 1 hard cheese, like aged cheddar
  • 1 soft cheese, like brie or goat cheese
  • 1 apple or pear, sliced
  • A small bunch of grapes
  • Something salty: salted almonds, pretzels or olives
  • Something sweet: dates, dried apricots or a drizzle of honey
  • A few sprigs of rosemary or thyme (optional)
  • A pitcher of water

Nothing on this board contains cannabis. If tonight includes a cannabis product, keep it in its labeled package, off the board and out of reach of kids and pets.

Make it

  1. 01

    Set the two cheeses on a board or large plate, apart from each other.

  2. 02

    Slice the hard cheese. Leave the soft one whole, with a knife next to it.

  3. 03

    Fan the crackers out around the cheeses.

  4. 04

    Fill the gaps with fruit, then the salty things, then the sweet ones. Put the honey in a small dish if you are using it.

  5. 05

    Tuck in the herbs, and set the water and glasses on the table before you sit down.

Plan your night

The board takes care of itself. A little planning takes care of the rest.

  1. 01

    Set out water

    A glass each, on the table before anything else.

  2. 02

    Decide your one serving

    If tonight includes cannabis, pick it before you start, read the label and stick to one serving. Start low, wait, and don't stack a second serving because you don't feel anything yet. Our label guide shows where the per-serving number sits.

  3. 03

    Keep it off the board

    Edibles can look a lot like snacks. Keep yours in its labeled package, away from the food and out of reach of kids and pets.

  4. 04

    Stay in

    That's the point of a night in. Don't drive after using cannabis. If someone needs to get home, call them a ride.

Something to sip? Both of our drink recipes are non-alcoholic. Pair the board with the McLean Ave Citrus Spritz or the Hudson Valley Cider Sipper, and keep alcohol out of any night that includes cannabis.

Board questions

Is anything on this board infused?

No. Every item is plain food. If you're having cannabis tonight, it stays in its own labeled package, and you decide your one serving before you sit down.

Why no chocolate or gummies on the board?

On a night that includes cannabis, we keep anything that looks like an edible off the board. Mix-ups happen. Dates, dried apricots and honey cover sweet without the guesswork.

Can I make it ahead?

Yes. Slice the hard cheese and fruit a few hours early, cover them and keep them cold. A squeeze of lemon keeps apple and pear slices from browning. Add the crackers last so they stay crisp.
