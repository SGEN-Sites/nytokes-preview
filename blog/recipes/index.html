How to read a label

Why we start from a store-bought drink

Home infusions are hard to dose. Butter, oil or syrup made in a home kitchen has no lab test and no label, so every spoonful is a guess.

A sealed THC seltzer or tonic is the opposite. Legal New York products are tested by licensed labs before they reach the shelf, and drinks list milligrams per serving and per package. Those are two very different numbers, so read both. The per-serving number is your dose.

How the recipes work

  1. Make the base

    Every recipe is complete without THC and without alcohol. Plenty of nights, that's the whole drink.

  2. Add one serving, if you want

    The two drink recipes show exactly where one labeled serving goes. One glass, one serving. The snack board stays non-infused.

  3. Wait before more

    Drinks come on faster than edibles, which makes it easier to stack them without meaning to. Finish one, wait at least thirty minutes, then decide.

Recipe questions

Can I add alcohol to these recipes?

No. Never mix cannabis with alcohol. Combining them makes both harder to judge. These recipes are non-alcoholic, and they stay that way.

What dose should I start with?

If you have never had a cannabis drink, start at 2.5 mg and sip it slowly. Plenty of people who think they want more find 2.5 mg is the dose they keep buying. Our post on microdosing explains why less can actually be more. Effects vary from person to person.

At Toke Westchester, 471 McLean Ave in Yonkers, and Toke Rockland, 2 S Central Ave in Spring Valley. Shop in store, order ahead for pickup or curbside, or get it delivered across Westchester and Rockland. 21+ with a valid government-issued ID.
