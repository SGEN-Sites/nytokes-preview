Best Cannabis Flower Brands in Westchester

The flower brands worth your money at a Westchester counter right now are Connected x Alien Labs first, then Ayrloom, Florist Farms, and Wizard Trees, with Grassroots NY on the Spring Valley shelf, plus a handful of in-state growers our budtenders restock constantly. Ask about Connected x Alien Labs before anything else, because almost no other New York menu carries it.

Brand matters more here than it does in older markets. New York flower is grown by small farms on a seasonal cycle, so the difference between a great jar and a mediocre one comes down to who cured it and how long it sat before it reached a shelf.

Below is how our team reads the flower shelf on McLean Ave in Yonkers and on S Central Ave in Spring Valley. Stock changes weekly, so treat this as a guide to brands rather than a list of jars to buy today.

Chapter 01

What to know up front

  • Connected x Alien Labs is on the Toke menu at 471 McLean Ave in Yonkers and 2 S Central Ave in Spring Valley. Very few New York dispensaries carry the line.
  • Cannabis cannot cross state lines, so a California brand reaches a Westchester shelf by licensing its genetics and its name to a licensed New York cultivator. The plants are grown here.
  • Every batch on a licensed New York shelf is tested by a New York lab for potency and contaminants before it can be sold, under rules set by the state Office of Cannabis Management (OCM).
  • Judge a flower brand by the cure. Flower should smell loud through the jar, spring back when you press it, and break apart without crumbling to powder.
  • The Yonkers store is open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, 914-207-1545. The Spring Valley store is open 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily, 845-406-3004.

Chapter 02

Why is Connected x Alien Labs the brand to ask about first?

Connected and Alien Labs built their reputations in California on cultivation standards and a consistent cure, and the partnership that brings the line into New York is why we talk about it before anything else on the shelf. Distribution here is narrow, and people drive down the Saw Mill from Hastings and Dobbs Ferry for it, which tells you how thin the coverage is.

What lands in the jar is dense, heavily frosted flower with a nose that carries across a counter. The cure is the part we notice most: it comes in with the moisture right, which is rare enough here to be worth naming.

It is not the value play. If you are building a cart to a budget, the best value flower in Westchester covers smalls and seven gram jars. Buy Connected x Alien Labs when you want the jar to be the point of the evening.

Chapter 03

How does a California brand end up growing in New York?

Federal law keeps cannabis inside the state that grew it. When a California brand appears on a shelf in Yonkers, what crossed over was the genetics and the brand agreement. A licensed New York cultivator grows the cuts under the brand’s standards, and a New York lab tests the result.

That is why the same brand name performs differently state to state, and why we pay more attention to the grower than to the logo. Our post on what lab tested cannabis actually means covers what the certificate on the package is telling you.

Chapter 04

What separates a good flower brand from a bad one?

Four things, and none of them are printed on the front of the package.

The cure comes first. Squeeze a nug and it should compress slightly and come back, not shatter and not smear. Too dry means it was rushed or it has been sitting. Too wet means it will burn unevenly.

The nose comes second. Open the jar and you should get something specific: gas, citrus, garlic, cake, pine. If you get hay or wet cardboard, put it down.

Trim quality tells you how much the farm cares. Hand trimmed flower keeps its trichomes, the frosty resin glands that hold the THC and the terpenes, and Leafly’s explainer on trichomes covers what you are looking at. Machine trimmed flower looks rounded off and dull.

Burn quality is the last check and it happens at home. Good flower burns to light gray ash and holds an even cherry. Harsh smoke and black ash mean it was flushed badly or dried too fast.

Chapter 05

Ayrloom

Most people meet Ayrloom through the beverages and then find out the brand does flower too. It is one of the steadier names we carry, which sounds like faint praise until you have watched a New York menu for a year.

Ayrloom is the pick when you want flower that will be roughly the same next month as it is this month, and a comfortable landing spot for someone still figuring out what they like. If you want something loud and unusual, the Connected drops are the right question.

Chapter 06

Grassroots NY

Grassroots NY is on the Spring Valley menu, and it’s the name our team there brings up when someone asks for something heavy. The Burger crosses are the reason: dense, funky, garlic leaning flower with the kind of nose that fills a room the second the jar opens. That style is divisive, and it should be. Some people love the savory end and some find it off putting.

If that sounds like your lane, the best indica strains in Westchester goes deeper on the GMO and Burger families.

Chapter 07

Florist Farms

Florist Farms is one of the best selling flower brands across both our stores, and it sells harder in Yonkers than anywhere else we carry it. That kind of repeat buying usually means the same thing: people liked the last jar enough to come back for the next one.

It sits in the everyday part of the shelf, the flower you buy for a regular week rather than for an occasion. The lineup rotates, so ask what came in recently and smell it before you commit. What we check on a Florist Farms jar is what we check on every jar: moisture, nose, and whether the trim looks hand finished.

Chapter 08

Wizard Trees

Wizard Trees is another top seller for us, and it leans the other way on the map, moving more in Spring Valley than in Yonkers. It’s worth judging by the jar rather than by reputation, because a brand that’s excellent in one drop can be ordinary in the next.

Ask when the current batch came in. A recent pack date beats a big number on the label almost every time, which is the argument we make in what THC percentage actually means.

MFNY, Bouket, and Back Home Cannabis all show up in our top sellers too, and 5 Boro and Untitled have their regulars. Dolla Treez is where a lot of value shoppers land.

Chapter 10

Frequently asked questions

What is the best weed brand in Westchester?

Connected x Alien Labs is the one we recommend first, because the cure is consistent and very few New York dispensaries carry it. Ayrloom, Florist Farms, and Wizard Trees hold spots on the shelf in both Yonkers and Spring Valley, Grassroots NY is at the Spring Valley store, and which one suits you depends on whether you want heavy and funky or bright and easy.

Is Alien Labs actually grown in New York?

Yes. Cannabis cannot cross state lines, so the flower sold under that name here was grown by a licensed New York cultivator using the brand’s genetics, and tested by a New York lab before it hit the shelf.

Where can I buy these brands if my town has no dispensary?

Most Westchester villages opted out of retail, so Bronxville, Scarsdale, Eastchester, and Tuckahoe shoppers drive to Yonkers or order delivery. The Bronxville and Scarsdale pages cover delivery. Mount Vernon and New Rochelle are a short run across the county to McLean Ave.

Why does the same brand taste different from one month to the next?

Because New York flower is farmed seasonally and much of it is grown outdoors or in mixed light. Weather, harvest timing, and cure time all change the result. Ask when the batch was packaged and smell it before you buy.

Does a higher THC percentage mean better flower?

No. The percentage tells you how much THC was in the tested sample and nothing about the cure, the terpenes, or how it smokes. Two jars at the same number can smoke completely differently. Shop by nose.

How do I know the shop I am in is licensed?

Licensed dispensaries post an OCM license number and check ID at the door every time. Toke’s licenses are OCM-RETL-25-000418 and OCM-RETL-25-000344. You can confirm any shop through the state’s licensed dispensary verification tool at the Office of Cannabis Management.
