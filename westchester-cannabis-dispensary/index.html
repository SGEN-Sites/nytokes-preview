Delivery Across Westchester

NY Tokes is a fully licensed New York dispensary, and Westchester is our home county. Our shop sits at 471 McLean Ave in southwest Yonkers, a few blocks off the Bronx line. Drivers cover lower Westchester from there, including places like Mount Vernon, Bronxville, Eastchester, the Rivertowns, New Rochelle and White Plains. Enter your address at checkout to confirm we reach you. We don’t deliver to the Bronx.

Westchester delivery runs 10am to 10:30pm Sun: Thu, 10am to 11pm Fri: Sat. Pick ASAP delivery or book a scheduled hourly window between 10 AM and 10:30 PM (11 PM Fridays and Saturdays), whichever fits your night. Orders arrive in unmarked vehicles, and you can track the driver in real time.

Not in the mood to wait? Order online and grab it at the counter, or pull up on McLean and we'll run it out to the car. Everything on the menu is lab-tested and METRC-tracked from seed to sale.

How Westchester Delivery Works

Three steps. Your zip code does most of the work.

Step One

Check your zip

Add to cart on the Yonkers menu and enter your address. Your zip confirms coverage and shows the available delivery windows.

Step Two

Pick ASAP or a time

Choose ASAP delivery or lock in a scheduled hourly window between 10 AM and 10:30 PM (11 PM Fridays and Saturdays). Westchester delivery runs every day.

Step Three

Meet the driver with ID

Track the driver in real time. Have your government-issued ID ready, since the purchaser has to be there to accept it.

Delivery minimums apply. Questions about your zone? Call the Yonkers shop at (914) 207-1545 or see the full delivery zone list.

Westchester Cannabis Delivery FAQs

The questions we field most from Westchester customers. Don't see yours? Call the shop at (914) 207-1545.

Does NY Tokes deliver cannabis in Westchester County?

Yes. We run cannabis delivery across lower Westchester from the Yonkers shop, including places like Mount Vernon and Bronxville in the south up through the Rivertowns, White Plains and Chappaqua. Westchester delivery runs 10am to 10:30pm Sun: Thu, 10am to 11pm Fri: Sat. You can take ASAP delivery or book a scheduled hourly window between 10 AM and 10:30 PM (11 PM Fridays and Saturdays).

Which Westchester towns and zip codes are in the delivery zone?

The current Westchester zone covers Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, White Plains, Yonkers, Bronxville, Eastchester, Tuckahoe, Pelham, Pelham Manor, Scarsdale, Hartsdale, Hastings-on-Hudson, Dobbs Ferry, Ardsley, Irvington, Tarrytown, Sleepy Hollow, Ossining, Croton-on-Hudson, Larchmont, Mamaroneck, Harrison, Rye, Rye Brook, Port Chester, Purchase, Elmsford, Valhalla, Hawthorne, Thornwood, Pleasantville, Armonk, Chappaqua and more. Every town has its own page in the grid above. Add to cart online and your zip confirms coverage and the delivery window.

Is there a delivery minimum or a delivery fee in Westchester?

There's a $75 minimum on delivery orders. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. For help with your specific Westchester zone, call the Yonkers shop at (914) 207-1545 and a team member will walk you through it.

Where is the NY Tokes dispensary in Westchester?

Our Westchester store is at 471 McLean Ave, Yonkers, NY 10705, in southwest Yonkers a few blocks from the Bronx line. It's open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, and the phone number is (914) 207-1545. You can shop in store, order online for in-store pickup, pull up for curbside, or have it delivered.

What do I need to place a Westchester delivery order?

You need to be 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID, and the purchaser has to be there to accept the delivery. No medical card is required. Veterans get 15% off with a DD-214 or VA ID, and registered New York State medical patients get 10% off with a valid card.

Is NY Tokes a licensed New York dispensary?

Yes. The Yonkers store operates under New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000418, and our Spring Valley store operates under OCM-RETL-25-000344. Everything on the shelf and on the delivery menu is lab-tested and METRC-tracked from seed to sale.
