Meet So Dope

So Dope is one of the brands our budtenders reach for most. It's New York flower and concentrates that punch above the price, which is why it earns steady space on our shelves.

Every So Dope drop is New York licensed, lab-tested, and METRC-tracked from seed to sale. You get real potency numbers, fresh flower that moves fast, and eighths that don't cost a special-occasion price.

It's also home to some of our favorite picks, like Harlem 21. When our team grabs something for themselves, it's often a So Dope jar.
