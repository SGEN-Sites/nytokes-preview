What is Blue Dream?

Blue Dream is one of the most popular strains in America for a reason: a sweet blueberry aroma, a balanced and gentle uplift, and a smooth ride that suits new and seasoned smokers alike. So Dope's tests at 17.22% THC with a high 1.88% terpene profile.

Effects & when to reach for it

  • Relaxed and happy
  • Beginner-friendly
  • Daytime or early evening

Flavor & aroma

  • Sweet blueberry
  • Berry and light herbal notes
  • Smooth, mellow smoke
