Spring Valley Dispensary FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most behind the counter. Don’t see yours? Call (845) 406-3004, we pick up.

Is NY Tokes a licensed dispensary in Spring Valley?

Yes. NY Tokes holds New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000344. Every product on our shelves is lab-tested, METRC-tracked from seed to sale, and fully compliant with NYS cannabis law. There are still unlicensed shops floating around Rockland, we're not one of them.

Where is the Spring Valley dispensary located?

We're at 2 S Central Ave, Spring Valley, NY 10977. Easy to reach from Route 59 and the Palisades Parkway, and central to Monsey, Nanuet, New City, Suffern, Pearl River and Nyack.

Do you deliver across Rockland County?

Yes. We deliver to most Rockland zip codes, including Monsey, Nanuet, New City, Suffern, Pearl River, West Nyack, Nyack, Pomona, Airmont, Chestnut Ridge and surrounding towns. Add to cart online and your zip will confirm coverage and the delivery window.

Do I need a medical marijuana card to shop?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21+ with a valid government-issued photo ID. NYS medical cardholders get 10% off every order on top of any current promotion.

What are your hours?

Open every day, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends and most holidays. Hours can shift on Christmas or Thanksgiving; check our Instagram @nytokes for the live update.

Can I order ahead for pickup or curbside?

Absolutely. Order online, choose pickup or curbside, and we'll have it ready when you arrive. Saves the line, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings.

How do I pay?

Cash, PIN-based debit, or our in-store ATM. Federal banking rules prevent credit-card transactions at any New York dispensary, our ATM has low fees.

How much cannabis can I buy in one trip?

Adult-use limits in New York are 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate per day. Our budtenders will help you stay within the rules and explain anything you're unsure about.

Is there parking at the Spring Valley shop?

Yes, there's plenty of parking right along Central Avenue. No meters, no garage, no stress.

What's nearby if I want to make an afternoon of it?

We're a short drive from the Palisades Center for shopping, Rockland Lake State Park for a hike, and dozens of restaurants along Route 59. Easy to combine the visit with the rest of your day.
