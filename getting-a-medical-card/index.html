Getting a medical card in New York

Short version: you don't need a medical card to shop at Toke. If you're a registered New York medical patient, bring your valid card and get 10% off.

Do you need a medical card to shop?

No. Our stores in Yonkers and Spring Valley are licensed adult-use retailers. Anyone 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID can shop, no medical card needed.

How New York's medical program works

New York's medical cannabis program is run by the NYS Office of Cannabis Management. Certification comes from a registered practitioner, not from a dispensary, so our budtenders can't certify you.

If you're wondering whether the medical program is right for you, talk to your health care provider. The state's medical cannabis page is the official place to start.

No card? Shop like everyone else

Bring a valid government-issued ID showing you're 21 or older and shop the same menu. If it's your first time, tell the budtender. No pressure. No upsell games. No judgment.

Rather not wait? Order ahead on either menu and pick up in store, pull up for curbside, or have it delivered. Here's how delivery works.

Medical card FAQ

Do I need a medical card to shop at Toke?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21+ with a valid government-issued ID. We also offer a 10% discount for registered NYS medical cannabis patients with a valid card.

Can I get the 10% off without a card?

No. The patient discount requires a valid New York State medical cannabis card. You can still shop at 21 or older, and it's the same menu.

Do I have to show my card every visit?

No. Show proof once, we'll remember.

Can Toke help me get certified?

No. Certification comes from a registered practitioner, not a dispensary. Talk to your health care provider, and see the NYS Office of Cannabis Management for program details.
