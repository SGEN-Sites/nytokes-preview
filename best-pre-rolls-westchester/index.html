Best Pre Rolls in Westchester

The best pre rolls in Westchester are packed with whole ground flower instead of trim, rolled tight enough to burn in a straight line, and fresh enough that the paper has not gone brittle. Brand names help narrow the list. Those three checks do more work than any label on the box.

This guide covers what separates a clean cone from a bad one, how singles compare to multi packs, and which New York grown brands our budtenders keep reaching for. Drops rotate weekly, so nothing here is a strain list that goes stale by next month.

Chapter 01

What to know up front

  • Toke‘s Yonkers store at 471 McLean Ave is open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, and the pre roll shelf turns over every week.
  • Whole flower pre rolls are ground from actual buds. Shake pre rolls are ground from trim and the small bits at the bottom of the bin. The box almost never tells you which, so ask.
  • New York’s daily adult use purchase limit is 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate, and pre rolls count against the flower side of that limit.
  • A half gram cone is about two sessions for most people, a full gram closer to three or four. Multi packs usually come as five small cones, not five full grams.
  • Both Toke stores are licensed by the New York State Office of Cannabis Management, OCM-RETL-25-000418 in Yonkers and OCM-RETL-25-000344 in Spring Valley.

Chapter 02

What makes a pre roll worth buying?

New York’s legal market is young, and pre rolls are where the quality gap shows up fastest. A cone is the easiest place to hide shake, stems, and the popcorn buds nobody wanted to sell by the eighth. The better New York growers do not do that, and you can taste it in the first two pulls.

What you are paying for is grind consistency and pack density. Ground too fine, it chokes the airflow and burns hot. Ground too coarse, it tunnels, one side racing ahead while half the joint falls out on your shirt. Neither problem is printed on the package.

Freshness is the other half. Product moves quickly through Westchester shops, so a six month old cone is less common here than you might expect. Check the pack date when one is printed.

Chapter 03

How can you tell a good pre roll before you light it?

Roll it between two fingers. It should feel evenly dense from tip to twist. A soft spot in the middle means it will canoe on you.

Look at the twist. A tight, clean one means whoever packed it was not rushing. A frayed, loose end usually means the flower inside is dry.

Smell the package. If citrus, gas, or black pepper comes through the packaging, the terpenes (the aromatic compounds that give each strain its smell and a lot of its feel) are still there. If you get hay, it has been sitting somewhere warm.

Ask to look at a pack before you buy at either store. Our post on how our budtenders help you find the right product covers how that conversation goes.

Chapter 04

Single cones, two packs, or multi packs: which should you buy?

Buy a single full gram cone when you are trying a strain you have never had. One gram is enough to know whether you like it, and you are not stuck with four more if you do not.

Two packs are the sweet spot for most of our regulars. You get a second one for the weekend without committing to a whole box.

Multi packs are the value play. Five small cones in a tin usually cost less per gram than a single, and they travel better than a jar and a grinder. The tradeoff is that small cones burn fast and some brands pack them looser. If you are shopping on price, our guide to value flower in Westchester covers where the same money goes further.

Chapter 05

Which pre roll brands do we reach for in Yonkers?

Jeeter is the one most people walk in asking for by name, and the rolls are consistent. Connected x Alien Labs and Wizard Trees show up less often because the drops are limited, and the pre rolls go quickly when they land. Very few New York menus carry Connected x Alien Labs at all.

On the New York grown side, Florist Farms, MFNY, and Back Home Cannabis all put real flower in their cones. So Dope is one our team reaches for regularly, it is grown in New York and lab tested, and it moves fast. 5 Boro and Ripped are worth a look when you want something less expensive.

An honest note on strain type: New York haze in a pre roll is hit or miss. Some of it is bright and peppery and some tastes like nothing. The indica leaning crosses coming out of New York right now are more reliable. Our take on indica versus sativa versus hybrid explains why the label on the box is a rough guide at best.

Chapter 06

What is the difference between a regular pre roll and an infused one?

A regular pre roll is ground flower in a paper cone. An infused pre roll adds concentrate, usually hash, live resin, or distillate, mixed into the flower or coated on the outside. Infused cones hit harder, cost more, and need relighting more often because the concentrate changes how the paper burns. Our guide to infused pre rolls in Westchester breaks down the three types and who each one suits.

Chapter 08

Why McLean Ave is an easy stop on the commute home

The Yonkers store sits at 471 McLean Ave, on the main commercial strip in southwest Yonkers. If you drive home on the Saw Mill or the Bronx River Parkway, it is a few minutes off either one. If you come down Central Park Avenue from Hartsdale, you are already sitting in that traffic, and a stop on McLean costs less time than the Central Ave crawl already took.

Metro-North riders are close on both lines. The Hudson Line runs up the river side of Yonkers, the Harlem Line runs up through Crestwood, Tuckahoe, and Bronxville, and McLean Ave sits south of both. A lot of our evening regulars got off a train, got in a car, and swung by before dinner.

We are open until 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, which covers most late commutes. Street parking runs along McLean and the side streets off it, and it fills up on Friday and Saturday evenings. Chapter 09

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy pre rolls in Westchester? Toke at 471 McLean Ave in Yonkers carries pre rolls 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday. It is a licensed New York adult use dispensary, so everything on the shelf is tested and tracked. Bring a government photo ID showing you are 21 or older.

What is the best type of pre roll for a beginner? A half gram cone from a brand that uses whole flower. Half a gram is manageable, and whole flower burns smoother than shake, which matters more when you are not used to it. Skip infused cones until you know how regular flower affects you.

Do pre rolls count toward New York’s purchase limit? Yes. Pre rolls count as flower, and New York’s adult use limit is 3 ounces of flower per day. A five pack of half gram cones is 2.5 grams, so you are nowhere near the ceiling on a normal purchase.

Are single strain pre rolls better than blends? Single strain cones taste more like the strain you picked, which is the point if you already know what you like. Blends are cheaper and less predictable in flavor. Neither is stronger by definition, potency comes from the flower itself.

How long do pre rolls stay good? A sealed pre roll holds up for several months in a cool, dark place. Once it is open the flower dries out and the smoke gets harsher within a couple of weeks. Multi pack tins hold up better than paper sleeves.

Can I get pre rolls delivered in Westchester? Yes, from the Yonkers store, with a real ETA and a text from the driver. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more. The person who placed the order has to be there with ID to accept it.
