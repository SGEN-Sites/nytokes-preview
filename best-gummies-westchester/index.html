Gummies are the most forgiving thing in a dispensary and the easiest thing to get wrong. Almost every bad edible story we hear starts with a second piece at minute forty, because nothing was happening yet.

This page covers how much to take, which gummies fit which night, and which to skip. Our Yonkers and Spring Valley menus rotate weekly, so check the live menu before you build a cart.

Chapter 01

What to know up front

  • New York caps adult-use edibles at 10 mg of THC per serving and 100 mg per package. If you moved to Westchester from Colorado or California, your old 25 mg reference point does not exist on a legal New York menu.
  • Fast acting gummies, which use nano-emulsion to speed absorption, usually land in 15 to 30 minutes. Standard gummies that pass through your liver first usually take 45 to 90 minutes.
  • Toke sells gummies at 471 McLean Ave in Yonkers, open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, about ten minutes from Bronxville, Tuckahoe, and Mount Vernon, and at 2 S Central Ave in Spring Valley off Route 59, open 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.
  • Every gummy on a menu licensed by the New York State Office of Cannabis Management has a batch lab test behind it and a QR code on the package. Scan it. The milligrams per piece are printed on the box, not the website.
  • Edibles run four to six hours, so the time you eat one matters more than the strain name. A 10 mg gummy at 9 PM is a different decision than the same gummy at 4 PM.

Chapter 03

Fast acting or standard: which gummy should you buy?

Fast acting gummies break THC into droplets small enough to absorb before your liver gets involved. You feel them sooner and the arc is shorter, which is the whole point for anyone who has panicked at minute fifty.

Standard gummies take longer and tend to run heavier once they land, because your liver converts THC into a compound that hits harder. For a Saturday with no alarm set, that is fine. For a Tuesday after the drive home up Central Park Avenue, it is a lot of commitment.

Our read: if you are new, buy fast acting. If you know your dose and want a long evening, standard gummies do it better and cost less per milligram. The edibles versus smoking comparison on our blog breaks the timing down further.

Chapter 04

What are ratio gummies and who are they for?

A ratio gummy pairs THC with another cannabinoid, usually CBD or CBN, in a stated proportion like 1:1 or 5:1. The label tells you the milligrams of each.

A 1:1 THC to CBD gummy at 5 mg each is what people ask for after trying an edible once, not enjoying it, and wanting another shot. The CBD takes some of the edge off without flattening the whole thing.

CBN blends get bought for evenings. Nobody selling cannabis in New York gets to tell you a gummy will put you to sleep, and we are not going to. What we can tell you is that CBN gummies sell hardest between 8 and 10 PM on a weeknight, and the people who buy them come back. Our post on cannabis for sleep, stress, and recovery is the honest version of that conversation.

Chapter 05

Are 10 mg gummies actually worth it?

For a daily consumer, yes. You get the most THC per dollar at 10 mg per piece, and if your tolerance is real, a 5 mg gummy wastes a trip.

For everyone else, a 10 mg gummy is the package you buy once, have a rough Saturday with, and then ration until spring. Buy 10 mg if you know from experience that 5 mg is not enough.

Chapter 08

Should you buy gummies or a THC drink?

Gummies win on dose control, price per milligram, and portability. Drinks land faster, and a can in your hand at a barbecue reads as a seltzer. Our guide to the best cannabis drinks in Westchester covers that category, including the Ayrloom beverage line.

Chapter 09

Frequently asked questions

How long do cannabis gummies take to kick in? Fast acting gummies usually start in 15 to 30 minutes. Standard gummies usually take 45 to 90 minutes because the THC passes through your liver first. Either way, wait at least an hour before a second piece. Taking more at minute forty is the most common edible mistake we see at the counter.

What is a good gummy dose for a first timer? Between 2.5 mg and 5 mg. Half of a 5 mg piece is a genuinely light starting point, and a full 5 mg piece is enough for most people who smoke occasionally. New York caps servings at 10 mg, which is a strong dose that most first timers should not start with.

Are gummies stronger than smoking? They last longer and build slower, and the same milligram amount often feels heavier than flower. Edible potency is measured in milligrams, not the percentage on a flower jar. Our post on what THC percentage actually means explains why the two numbers do not compare.

Where can I buy THC gummies in Westchester County? At a licensed dispensary. Toke is at 471 McLean Ave in Yonkers, open until 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, with delivery across lower Westchester. Check any shop against the state’s licensed dispensary verification tool before you buy, because unlicensed stores sell untested edibles with unreliable milligram counts.

Can I order gummies online for pickup? Yes. Order ahead, then pay in person with cash or debit. No New York dispensary takes credit cards, which is a federal banking rule. Bring government photo ID showing you are 21 or older, as New York’s cannabis law requires.

Which gummies are best for a first time edible? A fast acting 5 mg piece from a New York producer, with Ayrloom the line we hand out most. Eat one, set a timer for an hour, and have food in your stomach. Cut it in half if you want to be careful.
