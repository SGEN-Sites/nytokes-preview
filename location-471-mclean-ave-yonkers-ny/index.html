Yonkers Dispensary FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most behind the counter. Don’t see yours? Call (914) 207-1545, we pick up.

Is NY Tokes a licensed Yonkers dispensary?

Yes. NY Tokes holds New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000418. Every product on our shelves is lab-tested, METRC-tracked from seed to sale, and 100% compliant with NYS law. If you've seen unlicensed shops around Yonkers, this isn't one of them, we're the real deal.

Where is the Yonkers dispensary located?

We're at 471 McLean Ave, Yonkers, NY 10705, on the south side of Yonkers right near the Bronx border. We're easy to reach from Bronxville, Mount Vernon and most of southern Westchester.

Do I need a medical card to shop?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21+ with a valid government-issued ID. We also offer a 10% discount for registered NYS medical cannabis patients with a valid card.

What time does the Yonkers location open and close?

We're open 10:00 AM to 10:30 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM Friday and Saturday, including weekends and most holidays. Holiday hours can shift; check our Instagram @nytokes for live updates.

Do you deliver to the Bronx or NYC?

No. We deliver to Yonkers and lower Westchester from the Yonkers shop, including Yonkers ZIPs 10701, 10703, 10704, 10705 and 10710 and White Plains. Add to cart online and your zip will tell you if you're in coverage.

How much cannabis can I buy in one visit?

Adult-use customers can purchase up to 3 ounces of flower or 24 grams of concentrate per day under New York State law. Our staff will help you stay within your limit and explain anything you're not sure about.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Credit cards aren't accepted at NY dispensaries due to federal banking regulations. We take cash, debit, Dutchie Pay, AeroPay and Alleaves Pay, and there's an ATM in the shop with low fees. Most regulars bring cash to keep it simple.

What's the best time to come in?

Mornings (10am-noon) and late evenings (8pm to close) are usually the chillest. Weekends get busy after 4pm, order ahead online and skip the line.

Is there parking near 471 McLean Ave?

Yes, there's street parking on McLean Avenue and the side streets. Most customers find a spot within a block. We don't have a dedicated lot, but turnover is steady.
