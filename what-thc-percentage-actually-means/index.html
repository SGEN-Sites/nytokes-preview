What THC Percentage Actually Means

THC percentage is simply a measurement of how much THC is in a product by weight.

A flower labeled 25% THC means that 25% of the dry weight is THC. What it doesn’t tell you is how the product will actually feel once you use it.

THC percentage does not automatically tell you:

  • How strong the experience will feel
  • Whether it will hit fast or slow
  • If it will make you anxious or relaxed
  • If you’ll enjoy it at all

We see this every day at Toke Cannabis. Someone comes in focused only on the number, grabs the highest THC on the menu, and then comes back later saying, “That wasn’t really what I was looking for.”

Higher THC Doesn’t Always Mean a Better Experience

This surprises a lot of people, especially newer shoppers.

We’ve seen:

  • 18% THC flower feel incredibly strong
  • 30% THC flower feel flat or one-dimensional

That’s because THC is only one part of the experience.

What Actually Shapes Your High

  • Terpenes (which influence flavor and effects)
  • Freshness of the flower
  • Your tolerance level
  • Your mood, stress, and sleep
  • Whether you’ve eaten recently

That’s why at Toke New York, the first thing we usually ask isn’t about numbers. It’s:

“What are you trying to feel?”

The Yonkers and Spring Valley Perspective

Here’s what people actually ask us every day in our Yonkers and Spring Valley stores:

  • “I want something strong, but not anxiety-strong.”
  • “I just want to relax after work.”
  • “I don’t want to be stuck on the couch.”
  • “I haven’t smoked in years: I need help.”

Someone stopping by after a long commute through Yonkers is usually looking for something very different than someone settling in for a quiet night at home in Spring Valley. Even if the THC percentage is the same, the experience they want often isn’t.

That’s why focusing only on THC numbers misses the bigger picture.

What You Should Look At Instead

When shopping at Toke Cannabis, think of THC percentage as a starting point, not the final answer.

A General Guideline (Not a Rule)

  • New or occasional users: 10: 18% THC
  • Regular users: 18: 25% THC
  • Higher tolerance users: 25%+ THC (but terpene profile still matters)

Beyond that, ask about:

  • Flavor profiles
  • Terpenes
  • How the product tends to make people feel

Being honest about what you want helps us guide you toward something you’ll actually enjoy.

The Bottom Line

THC percentage is just a number not a guarantee.

The best cannabis experience comes from:

  • Knowing what you’re looking for
  • Asking questions
  • Paying attention to how products actually make you feel

And if you’re ever unsure, that’s exactly why we’re here.
