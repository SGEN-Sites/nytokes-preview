Weed Delivery Across Rockland, NY

Rockland is our home county. The Spring Valley shop sits a half-mile off Route 59, and our drivers work out of it all day, so you get a delivery window you can plan around. Rockland delivery runs 10am to 11pm, every day.

Pick ASAP if you want it now, or choose a scheduled hourly slot between 10AM and 11PM if you would rather plan around your evening. Orders ride in unmarked vehicles and you can track yours in real time through our OnFleet integration. There's a $75 minimum, and your zip code confirms coverage and the window when you add to cart. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more.

Licensed and lab-tested

Seven days a week

NY Tokes Spring Valley

Rockland County · Licensed NYS Dispensary

Rockland FAQ

Questions From Around the County

The ones we hear most on the phone. If yours is not here, call (845) 406-3004 and we pick up.

Do you deliver cannabis across Rockland County?

Yes. We deliver to 40 Rockland towns and hamlets from our licensed Spring Valley dispensary, including Monsey, Nanuet, New City, Suffern, Pearl River, Nyack, West Nyack, Pomona, Airmont and Chestnut Ridge. Add items to your cart and your zip code confirms coverage and the delivery window.

What are the Rockland delivery hours?

Rockland delivery runs 10am to 11pm, every day. You can choose ASAP or pick a scheduled hourly window between 10AM and 11PM.

How much does delivery cost in Rockland County?

There's a $75 delivery minimum. Delivery is free on orders of $75 or more.

Where is the dispensary that Rockland deliveries come from?

2 S Central Ave, Spring Valley, NY 10977, about a half-mile from Route 59 and minutes from the Palisades Parkway. The shop is open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and there is parking right along Central Avenue.

Is NY Tokes a licensed New York dispensary?

Yes. Our Spring Valley shop holds New York State Office of Cannabis Management retail license OCM-RETL-25-000344. Everything on the shelf is lab-tested and METRC-tracked from seed to sale.

What do I need when the driver arrives?

A valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. The purchaser has to be present to accept the delivery. Veterans get 15% off with a DD-214 or VA ID, and NYS medical cardholders get 10% off.

Which Rockland towns are closest to the Spring Valley shop?

Monsey is about 5 minutes out, Airmont and Chestnut Ridge about 8, Nanuet about 10, New City and Pomona about 12, and Suffern and Pearl River about 15. Nyack runs closer to 18 minutes and Stony Point about 25.
